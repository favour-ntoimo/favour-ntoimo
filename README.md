## 👩‍⚖️ About Me

> *"In law, we argue from evidence. In data, I'm learning to let the evidence speak."*

I'm a **lawyer** at the beginning of my journey into **data analysis**. My legal training taught me to read carefully, question assumptions, look for inconsistencies and reason from evidence. I'm now learning to apply those same habits to data, and this GitHub is where I share my progress.

- ⚖️ **Background:** law, with training in legal research, careful reading of documents and structured argument
- 📊 **Learning:** SQL, Power BI and Excel through hands-on projects
- 🌱 **Right now:** completing guided projects and building my first portfolio
- 🎯 **Goal:** to grow into a data analyst who combines legal thinking with analytical skills
- 🤝 **Open to:** learning opportunities, internships, entry-level roles, mentorship and collaboration

---

## 🧩 How My Legal Background Helps

I'm new to data, but some habits from law carry over naturally:

| ⚖️ From Law | 📊 Applied to Data |
|---|---|
| Examining evidence before drawing conclusions | Checking data for errors before analysing it |
| Building arguments step by step | Writing queries and analysis in a logical order |
| Attention to detail in documents | Spotting inconsistencies, such as mislabelled records |
| Explaining complex matters simply | Presenting findings clearly in dashboards and reports |
| Respect for confidentiality | Handling data responsibly |

### 🔭 Where I'd Like to Grow
As I build my skills, I'm especially interested in where data and law overlap: **legal and compliance analytics**, **contract and case tracking**, **data privacy and governance**, and **data for social impact**.

## 🚀 My Projects

### 📊 [TNMT Revenue Dashboard | Power BI](https://github.com/favour-ntoimo/TNMT-data-analysis-with-Power-BI)
My first Power BI dashboard, summarising revenue, profit, top products, sales teams and states for a retail dataset.
`Power BI` `DAX` `Power Query`
- Connected four tables into a simple data model
- Built KPI cards, trend charts and a product category slicer
- Noticed that many sales records weren't linked to a sales team, a useful lesson in checking data quality

### 💧 [A Data-Driven Journey in Maji Ndogo | SQL](https://github.com/favour-ntoimo/Data-driven_Journey_In_Maji_Ndogo)
A guided SQL project exploring and cleaning a water-access survey database.
`MySQL` `Data Cleaning` `Exploration`
- Explored eight related tables and the data dictionary
- Found wells marked "Clean" despite contamination readings
- Practised fixing records safely on a test copy before applying changes
